A redesign around the shopping journey

Using a security-product store as the design context, I independently developed brand and audience analysis, desktop wireframes, a visual style guide, and desktop and mobile designs. The project explores how shoppers understand products, choose a suitable setup, and find ongoing support.

This is a conceptual course redesign, with no real client engagement with Amcrest. Product information, pricing, reviews and brand figures shown in the mockups are design content.

Design goal: a clear starting point for different shoppers

The design considers both shoppers who know the specifications they need and newcomers who start with “what do I want to protect?” The goal is to connect discovery, comparison and purchase intent in a coherent journey.

Families & remote care

Understand devices through people, spaces and coverage needs.

Small businesses

Find a path toward multi-camera setups and complete systems.

People living alone

Organize information around entryways, indoor monitoring and ease of setup.

Four key design decisions

01

Two paths to discovery

Use home, business, door and vehicle scenarios for needs-led discovery, while retaining categories and filters for shoppers with a specific product in mind.

02

A purposeful homepage sequence

Start with a lived scenario, then introduce trust cues, real-life usage demonstrations and recommended products to connect needs with shopping actions.

03

Extend the journey into service

Bring product details, buying guidance, cloud plans, support and account information into one structure so users can find a next step at different stages.

04

Reorganize for mobile

Adapt navigation, card arrangements, image–text relationships and step sequences so desktop content retains its hierarchy on smaller screens.

The core journey covered by the design

  1. Home
  2. Shop by need / category
  3. Product details
  4. Cart overlay

Search, cloud service, support and account pages extend this journey. The demo connects this complete shopping path.
